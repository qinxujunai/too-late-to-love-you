The story

She does not leave the photograph at sea

After the breakup she takes the ferry home with the only photograph of the two of them. She cries in the cabin, looks at the picture on deck, and smiles once through her tears.

She does not tear it up or throw it into the sea. She puts it away, breathes in the sea wind, and walks off the ferry towards a door she cannot see beyond.

Making

It didn’t start with a script

There was only a ten-second shot. The story grew out of generating, rejecting and editing, again and again.

  1. 01

    Ten seconds before a story

    First came warm cabin light, a gust of wind and one glance. There was no story yet. Only after rounds of discussion and test shots with AI did it become clear: she is taking the ferry home.

    Watch this moment 00:00.00 ›

  2. 02

    One photograph decides the relationship

    The first version showed an elderly couple, and the feeling went wrong at once. A young couple’s small faces came out distorted and aged. In the end, only their backs, leaning together by the sea: no faces, and still unmistakably in love.

    Watch this moment 00:21.50 ›

  3. 03

    Carry the tears across the cut

    The second shot was redone the most: it crossed the line, the light went grey, the crying went too far. The fix was to start from the previous shot’s actual last frame and describe only what happens next. The coat, the light and the tears all carried over.

    Watch this moment 00:11.20 ›

  4. 04

    Even the favourite opening was redone

    The opening had morning tones that didn’t match the afternoon sun after it, and she was sitting on the wrong side. Five approaches were tried; one made her stand when she had to sit. Using the opening’s own frames as reference finally fixed it.

    Watch this moment 00:07.50 ›

  5. 05

    A beautiful aerial shot can still go

    Cutting straight to a wide shot of the ferry was spectacular, and felt like another film. Instead the camera pulls back from her in stages: her, then the cabin door, then the whole sea. The wide view now has somewhere to come from.

    Watch this moment 00:44.80 ›

  6. 06

    Don’t turn the unknown into an answer

    A seaside walkway ending was prettier, and it was cut anyway: it said too clearly where she goes. She walks towards a door. What lies beyond it, we don’t know. That is enough.

    Watch this moment 00:56.40 ›

It all started with one prompt

A cinematic 10–second video of a beautiful young Asian woman on a moving ferry, wearing a beige trench coat and a thick oversized plaid scarf. Natural sunlight streams through the cabin windows, reflecting off the water outside. The camera starts with a medium shot, showing the melancholic and moody atmosphere of the boat cabin with blurred passengers in the background. The camera slowly pushes in for an extreme, highly detailed close–up of the woman's face. The wind gently blows her messy hair across her face, highlighting her melancholic, deep, and thoughtful eyes. No speaking. In the final seconds, she slowly turns her head to look over her shoulder directly at the camera with a profound gaze.
